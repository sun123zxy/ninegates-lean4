4 Interior positivity and deletion

Throughout this chapter, the start \(j\) of a deleted block is zero-based: the removed mathematical ranks are \(j+1,j+2,j+3\). The hypotheses \(1\leq j\) and \(j+3{\lt}n\) say exactly that all three removed ranks are internal.

  • Ninegates.interior_positive

[Interior coordinates are positive] Source: half-formal.qmd, Lemma #lem-interior-positive. Let \(n\geq 6\) with \(3\mid n\). If \(c\) is a live all-rank wait, then \(c_k\geq 1\) for every internal rank \(2\leq k\leq n-1\).

Proof ▶

Fix an internal \(k\) and suppose \(c_k=0\). Let \(A\) and \(B\) be the total multiplicities strictly left and strictly right of \(k\). Since a run crossing the cut would contain rank \(k\), every complete decomposition after a draw separates into left and right pieces, with the unique pair on exactly one side.

Drawing on the left shows

\[ (A,B)\equiv (1,0)\ \text{or}\ (2,2)\pmod3, \]

and drawing on the right shows

\[ (A,B)\equiv (2,2)\ \text{or}\ (0,1)\pmod3. \]

Hence \(A\equiv B\equiv 2\pmod3\). It follows that after every left-side draw the left piece is purely meldable. If the left side had two ranks, drawing its first and second ranks would produce two meldable vectors whose rank sums differ by \(1\), contradicting Lemma 17. Thus the left side has at most one rank. The symmetric argument gives the same conclusion on the right. Therefore \(n\leq 3\), contradicting \(n\geq 6\).

  • Ninegates.delete_three_live_wait

[Deletion preserves a live all-rank wait] Source: half-formal.qmd, Lemma #lem-delete. Let \(n\geq 9\), \(3\mid n\), and let \(c\) be a live all-rank wait. If the three internal coordinates beginning at \(j\) all have multiplicity \(1\), then \(\operatorname {del}_j(c)\) is a live all-rank wait of length \(n-3\).

Proof ▶

The deleted coordinates contain exactly three tiles, so the shortened total is \((n+4)-3=(n-3)+4\). Every retained coordinate still has multiplicity at most \(3\).

Fix a new draw \(x'\), and let \(x=\iota _j(x')\). Choose a complete decomposition of \(c+e_x\), with pair at \(p\) and meldable remainder \(d\). Since \(x\) is retained, the three deleted coordinates still have multiplicity \(1\), so the pair cannot lie among them. Hence \(p=\iota _j(p')\) for a unique retained \(p'\). Coordinatewise calculation gives

\[ \operatorname {del}_j(d) =\operatorname {del}_j(c)+e_{x'}-2e_{p'}. \]

The left side is meldable by Lemma 85. Thus every draw completes the shortened hand, proving the live-wait condition.

[Deletion detects the standard hand] Source: half-formal.qmd, final assertion of Lemma #lem-delete. If the deleted block is internal and consists of three singleton coordinates, then

\[ c=G_n\quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \operatorname {del}_j(c)=G_{n-3}. \]
